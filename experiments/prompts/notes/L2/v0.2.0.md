- API `Device location verification API`, served under `/location-verification/v0`. One operation: `verifyLocation`, `POST` to `/verify`.
- The body requires `device` and `area`; `maxAge` is optional.
- `device` needs at least one of `phoneNumber`, `networkAccessIdentifier`, `ipv4Address`, `ipv6Address` (`minProperties` 1). `phoneNumber` matches `^\+?[0-9]{5,15}$`, so the `+` is optional.
- `area` has `areaType` `CIRCLE`, a `center` with `latitude` and `longitude`, and a `radius`.
- `radius` is an integer in meters, from 2000 to 200000.
- `maxAge` is an integer in seconds with a minimum of 60.
- The response has the required `verificationResult` (`TRUE`, `FALSE`, `UNKNOWN`, `PARTIAL`), plus the optional `lastLocationTime` and `matchRate` (1 to 99).
- Error responses: `400`, `401`, `403`, `404`, `500`, `503`.
